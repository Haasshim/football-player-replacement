  What is a 'Home Grown Player'?


  A "Home Grown Player" means a player who, irrespective of nationality or age, has been registered with any club affiliated to The Football Association or the Football
  Association of Wales for a period, continuous or not, of three entire seasons, or 36 months, before his 21st birthday (or the end of the season during which he turns 21).

  A club’s Under-21 players are not included on the 25-man squad lists and are eligible to play in the Premier League.

  What constitutes an Under-21 player?


  For the 2026/27 Premier League season, Under-21 players will have been born on or after 1 January 2007.
